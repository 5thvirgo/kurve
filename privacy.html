Kurve Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: everything you enter in Kurve stays on your iPhone. We don't have servers that receive your health data, we don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell or share your information.

What Kurve stores

To work, Kurve saves what you type in, only on your device:

  • Your first name, medicine, dose, usual shot day and time, and pen supply.
  • Shots you log: date, dose and injection site.
  • Daily check-ins: side effects, cravings score and, if you choose, your weight.

This is health information, so we treat it carefully. It is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, under Apple's protections.

What we receive

Nothing. Kurve does not send your shots, check-ins, weight or name to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no sign-in.

Reminders

Shot reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. They are not sent through any server. You can turn them off anytime in Settings › Notifications › Kurve.

Purchases

Kurve Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. Apple's own privacy policy covers your purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing with your doctor

The doctor report is created on your phone. It only leaves your phone if you choose to send it, using Messages, Mail or another app you pick.

Deleting your data

In Kurve, open Settings and tap Erase all my data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing left with us to delete.

Children

Kurve is intended for adults using prescribed medication and is not directed at children under 16.

Not medical advice

Kurve is a tracking tool. Medicine levels and forecasts are estimates. Always follow your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If Kurve ever starts sending data off your phone (for example, an optional backup), we'll explain it here and ask you first in the app.
